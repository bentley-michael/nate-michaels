Why Coffee Geography Matters More Than You Think

How elevation, latitude, climate, trade routes, and processing shape the cup in front of you.

Coffee is one of the easiest everyday products to forget is geographic. Before it becomes a drink, it is a crop shaped by altitude, rainfall, temperature, labor, processing, infrastructure, trade, and distance. A cup of coffee carries a surprising amount of landscape with it.

That landscape is not just a backdrop. It helps determine what can be grown, how it is moved, and the work required to bring it from a farm to a cafe or kitchen table.
